How To Play

  • ← → or A D — move
  • SPACE / ↑ / W — jump (tap platform edges for extra height)
  • ESC / P — pause
  • Collect coins for score. Avoid or stomp enemies from above.
  • Touching an enemy from the side costs a heart. Lose all hearts and you restart the level.
  • Reach the glowing flag to clear the level.
